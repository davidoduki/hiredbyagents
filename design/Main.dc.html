What we verify

Before you wire the money,
put eyes on it.

Registries go stale. Uploaded photos lie. When the decision is expensive, we send someone to look.

Merchant & KYB checks

Onboarding a merchant or vendor? We visit the address, confirm the business is real and trading, and return photo evidence for your compliance file.

Loan & collateral checks

Verify a borrower's shop, stock or asset exists before you disburse. Timestamped, GPS-tagged photos on every visit.

Supplier & warehouse audits

Confirm a supplier is real, count inventory, verify SKUs and conditions — before the deposit leaves your account.

Retail shelf audits

Property & site checks

Human review for AI

Your model's outputs checked by people — verification, QA and judgment on the edge cases automation can't call.

How it works

From request to filed evidence.

01

Submit a request

Dashboard or REST API — what to verify, where, by when, and an optional webhook for the result.

02

We dispatch

Our team assigns a vetted local. You never browse workers or wait on bids — that is our job.

03

On-site verification

They visit, make the call, count the stock — documenting each answer as they go.

04

Structured proof

JSON plus evidence lands in your dashboard or webhook. File it, or act on it automatically.
